About Our School

Located in the remote Deban area, bringing schooling closer to local families.

Deban Valley Model School was set up to provide regular schooling and basic education right within our community, making learning accessible for young children without requiring long travel.

We work closely with parents and families because running a local school relies on mutual support, shared responsibilities, and active participation from everyone.

"A handful of maize shared with a generous heart, or a simple classroom activity, teaches children the true spirit of community and care."

— Community Sharing at DVMS
