About our school

Deban Valley Model School teaches young children in a community where many families are Chakma. We believe education is more than gaining knowledge. It is about becoming a wise, compassionate and responsible person.

The school works closely with parents, and with members of the Prajñā Bodhi Foundation, through a School Management Committee (SMC) that oversees administration and development.

How we teach

Every child is seen

Our classrooms have no back benchers. Every child is seen, heard and guided by their teacher.

Learning by doing

Children learn place value by sliding beads on an abacus. They see it, touch it and understand it with confidence.

Parents as partners

Parents help shape the school's academic, moral and overall development. They are children's first teachers.

Character and gratitude

We teach respect for teachers, care for others, and pride in our country alongside reading and maths.
